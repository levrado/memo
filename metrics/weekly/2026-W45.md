## Challenges & Learnings

- **The recap cadence collided with a burst of merges.** The W44 recap was drafted at ~22:00 UTC Nov 1, but 8 of the week's 11 PRs merged after that. A recap written at end-of-week misses same-day-night activity; this overlapping W45 report is the correction, and the lesson is that the "week" boundary is arbitrary when the factory merges around the clock.
- **Post-merge verification earned its keep.** The offline banner passed review but the UI Verifier caught a real visual defect (message overflow at 375px) within hours, filed #23, and the fix merged the next morning — a clean demonstration of the ship → verify → fix loop.
- **CI bootstrap instability isn't fully over.** Nov 1 saw a 50% pass rate and first pushes still occasionally need `chore: re-trigger CI` commits. One visual-regression spec (`database-galleryview--default`) flakes on external image fetches — flagged as environmental, still unresolved.
- **The hardest blocker is still credentials, not code.** The deploy-migrations failure (#21) and the two dead automations (#9, #11) all wait on human-provided secrets. The factory can ship features but can't fix missing configuration.
